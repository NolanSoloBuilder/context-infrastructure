## 提交描述

BeamJoin is a browser-based design workspace for custom aluminum extrusion projects. Build workbenches, equipment stands, shelving, and other modular frames with an AI assistant and a visual 3D editor.

Start from an idea or an example, review proposed changes, and refine dimensions, connectors, panels, and machining details. Keep the design connected to practical material information, including profile specifications, cut lengths, quantities, and panel details. Export PDF and Excel documents to support sourcing and fabrication.

Built for makers, DIY builders, and small workshops. Basic manual editing and exports are free; AI features use credits.
