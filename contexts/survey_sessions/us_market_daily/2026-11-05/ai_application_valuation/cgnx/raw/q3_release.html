      Cognex Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

      News provided by

      Cognex Corporation

      Nov 04, 2026, 16:30 ET

      Share this article

        •

      Share this article

        •

      NATICK, Mass., Nov. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Cognex Corporation (NASDAQ: CGNX), the global technology leader in industrial machine vision, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended October 4, 2026.

      Third-Quarter Financial and Operating Highlights

      • Achieved record quarterly revenue of $313 million, an increase of 13% year over year, driven by broad-based strength across most major end markets; excluding the Q3 2025 one-time benefit from the commercial partnership with a medical lab automation channel partner (the "Commercial Partnership" or "CP"), revenue grew 19% year over year.
      • Operating margin was 32.2%; delivered an Adjusted EBITDA margin of 35.5%, up 1,060 basis points year over year. Excluding the CP, Adjusted EBITDA margin expanded 1,340 basis points year over year, marking the ninth consecutive quarter of margin expansion.
      • Net income per diluted share was $0.50; generated Adjusted diluted earnings per share of $0.53, up 61% year over year, representing the ninth consecutive quarter of growth. Excluding the CP, Adjusted EPS (diluted) increased 89% year over year.
      • Increased full-year 2026 guidance; continue to anticipate strong double-digit revenue growth and year-over-year expansion in profitability.
      • Announced the entry into a definitive agreement to acquire RealSense, Inc., a leader in depth-sensing cameras and vision technology for robotic perception and physical artificial intelligence (AI). The all-cash transaction is expected to close during the fourth quarter of 2026.
      • Announced the launch of the In-Sight® 1750, a next-generation wafer and panel identification system designed for the Semiconductor industry, one of the most demanding automation markets.
      • Cognex to host 2027 Investor Day in June at its Natick, MA headquarters.

      "Our third-quarter results reflect strong execution across the business, with record revenue, broad-based growth across most of our major end markets, and continued progress against our strategic objectives," said Matt Moschner, President and CEO. "We believe we are strengthening our leadership in AI-powered machine vision through innovation, expanding our long-term growth opportunity through the announced acquisition of RealSense, and continuing to help position Cognex to benefit from what we see as powerful trends in automation, robotics, and artificial intelligence."

      Dennis Fehr, CFO, added, "We believe Q3 was another strong proof point for our profitable growth strategy. Record revenue, significant margin expansion, and Adjusted EBITDA flow-through above 100% demonstrate the progress we have made transforming our operating model. Our strong balance sheet and disciplined capital allocation strategy enabled us to pursue the RealSense acquisition, while retaining flexibility to invest in both organic and inorganic growth opportunities with the potential to create further value for shareholders."

      • Revenue was $313 million, compared with $277 million in the second quarter of 2025, an increase of 13%, driven by broad-based strength across most major end markets. Excluding the one-time benefit from the Commercial Partnership, revenue grew 19% year over year. Foreign currency exchange did not have a meaningful impact on consolidated revenue in the quarter.

      • Gross margin was 70.8% compared to 67.6% in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted gross margin was 71.3% compared to 68.4% in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 290 basis points. The year-over-year increase was primarily driven by favorable mix, higher volume, and faster-than-expected pricing execution. These benefits more than offset the expected memory cost headwinds in the quarter.
      • Operating expenses were $121 million compared to $130 million in the third quarter of 2025, a decrease of 7%. Adjusted operating expenses were $116 million compared to $125 million in the third quarter of 2025, a decrease of 7%, primarily reflecting continued cost management activities, somewhat offset by higher sales commissions.
      • Operating income was $101 million compared to $58 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 74%. Operating margin was 32.2% compared to 20.9% in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 1,130 basis points. Adjusted operating margin was 34.2% compared to 23.2% in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 1,100 basis points.
      • Adjusted EBITDA was $111 million compared to $69 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 61%. Adjusted EBITDA margin was 35.5% compared to 24.9% in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 1,060 basis points. Excluding the one-time benefit from the Commercial Partnership, Adjusted EBITDA margin increased 1,340 basis points year over year, driven by revenue growth, strong Adjusted Gross Margin and disciplined cost management.
      • Net income of $85 million compared to $18 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 372%. Adjusted net income of $90 million compared to $56 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 61%.
      • Net income per diluted share was $0.50 compared to $0.10 in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 400%. Adjusted diluted earnings per share were $0.53 compared to $0.33 in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 61%. Excluding the one-time benefit from the Commercial Partnership, Adjusted diluted earnings per share increased 89% year over year.

      Balance Sheet and Cash Flow Highlights

      • As of October 4, 2026, Cognex's financial position remained strong, with $846 million in cash and investments and no debt.
      • During the third quarter, Cognex generated $106 million of cash from operating activities compared to $87 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 22%.
      • During the third quarter, Cognex generated Free Cash Flow (FCF) of $104 million compared to $86 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 21%. Third quarter FCF conversion rate was 122% of net income and 116% of Adjusted net income. Trailing twelve-month FCF conversion rate was 118% of net income and 106% of Adjusted net income.
      • Cognex paid $14 million in dividends to shareholders in the third quarter.

      Dividend

      On November 4, 2026, Cognex's Board of Directors declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.09 per share, representing an increase of $0.005 per share, or approximately 6%, over the $0.085 per share dividend paid in the prior quarter. The dividend is payable on December 3, 2026, to all shareholders of record at the close of business on November 19, 2026.

      Non-GAAP Financial Measures

      This press release includes certain non-GAAP financial measures, including adjusted gross profit and margin, adjusted operating expense, adjusted operating income and margin, adjusted EBITDA and margin, adjusted net income, adjusted earnings per share of common stock, diluted, adjusted effective tax rate, and free cash flow and free cash flow conversion rate. Cognex defines its non-GAAP metrics as follows:

      • Adjusted gross profit and margin: Gross margin adjusted for amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets, as well as, if applicable, restructuring charges, reorganization charges, acquisition and integration costs and one-time discrete events.
      • Adjusted operating expense: Operating expense adjusted for amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets, as well as, if applicable, restructuring charges, reorganization charges, acquisition and integration costs and one-time discrete events.
      • Adjusted operating income and margin: Operating income adjusted for amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets, as well as, if applicable, restructuring charges, reorganization charges, acquisition and integration costs and one-time discrete events.
      • Adjusted EBITDA and margin: Operating income adjusted for amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets and depreciation, as well as, if applicable, restructuring charges, reorganization charges, acquisition and integration costs and one-time discrete events.
      • Adjusted net income: Net income adjusted for amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets, as well as, if applicable, restructuring charges, reorganization charges, acquisition and integration costs, discrete tax items, tax impact on reconciling items and one-time discrete events (such as loss on sale of business).
      • Adjusted earnings per share of common stock, diluted: Adjusted net income divided by diluted weighted average common and common-equivalent shares.
      • Adjusted effective tax rate: Effective tax rate adjusted for discrete tax items and the net impact of the other non-GAAP adjustments.
      • Free cash flow: Cash provided by operating activities less cash for capital expenditures.
      • Free cash flow conversion rate: Free cash flow divided by net income or adjusted net income, as applicable.

      Cognex may disclose results on a constant-currency basis as one measure to evaluate its performance and compare results between periods as if the exchange rates had remained constant period-over-period.

      Cognex believes these non-GAAP financial measures are helpful because they allow investors to more accurately compare results over multiple periods using the same methodology that management employs in its budgeting process, in its review of operating results, and for forecasting and planning for future periods. Cognex's definitions may differ from the definitions used by other companies and therefore comparability may be limited. In addition, other companies may not publish these or similar metrics. Furthermore, these measures have certain limitations in that they do not include the impact of certain non-recurring expenses that are reflected in our consolidated statement of operations that are necessary to run our business. Thus, our non-GAAP financial measures should be considered in addition to, not as substitutes for, or in isolation from, measures prepared in accordance with GAAP.

      Please see the section "Reconciliation of Selected Items from GAAP to Non-GAAP" below for more detailed information regarding non-GAAP financial measures herein, including the items reflected in our adjusted financial metrics and a description of these adjustments.

      Description of adjustments:

      In addition to reporting financial results in accordance with U.S. GAAP, the Company also provides various non-GAAP measures that incorporate adjustments for the impacts of special items. Adjustments incorporated in the preparation of these non-GAAP measures for the periods presented include the items described below:

      Depreciation:

      • The company incurs expense related to its normal use of property, plant and equipment.

      Acquisition and integration costs:

      • The Company has incurred charges related to the purchase and integration of acquired businesses. During the periods presented, these costs were primarily related to the ongoing integration of Moritex Corporation, which the company acquired in the fourth quarter of 2023.

      Amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets:

      • The Company excludes the amortization of acquired intangible assets from non-GAAP expense and income measures. These items are inconsistent in amount and frequency and are significantly impacted by the timing and size of acquisitions, and include the amortization of customer relationships, completed technologies, and trademarks that originated from prior acquisitions. The largest driver of intangible asset amortization was the acquisition of Moritex Corporation.

      Reorganization charges:

      • The Company has incurred charges related to the reorganization of its employees. During the three-month period ended October 4, 2026, these costs consisted primarily of severance and consulting fees.

      Loss on sale of business:

      • The Company has recognized a pre-tax loss related to the divestiture of its Japan-focused trading business, which includes direct costs associated with the divestiture incurred during the nine-month period ended October 4, 2026.

      Losses on sale of investments to fund acquisition:

      • The Company has recognized a pre-tax loss related to the sale of investments, primarily corporate bonds, during the three-month period ended October 4, 2026. The proceeds from these sales, along with existing cash balances, are intended to fund the acquisition of RealSense that is expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026.

      Discrete tax (benefit) expense and tax impact of reconciling items:

      • Items unrelated to current period ordinary income or (loss) that generally relate to changes in tax laws, adjustments to prior period's actual liability determined upon filing tax returns, adjustments to previously recorded reserves for uncertain tax positions, establishments and adjustments of valuation allowances, stock based compensation, and adjustments to deferred tax positions.

      • We estimate the tax effect of items identified in the reconciliation by applying the statutory tax rate to the pre-tax amount.

      About Cognex Corporation

      For over 40 years, Cognex has been making advanced machine vision easy, paving the way for manufacturing and distribution companies to become faster, smarter, and more efficient through automation. Innovative technology in our vision sensors and systems solves critical manufacturing and distribution challenges, providing unparalleled performance across a diverse set of end markets, including logistics, consumer electronics, automotive, packaging, and semiconductor.

      Cognex makes these tools more capable and easier to deploy thanks to a longstanding focus on AI, helping factories and warehouses improve quality and maximize efficiency without needing highly technical expertise. We are headquartered near Boston, USA, with locations in over 30 countries and more than 30,000 customers worldwide. Learn more at cognex.com.
