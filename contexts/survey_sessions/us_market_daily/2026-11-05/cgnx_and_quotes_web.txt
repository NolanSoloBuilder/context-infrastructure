L167: Third-Quarter Financial and Operating Highlights
L168:   * Achieved record quarterly revenue of $313 million, an increase of 13% year over year, driven by broad-based strength across most major end markets; excluding the Q3 2025 one-time benefit from the commercial partnership with a medical lab automation channel partner (the "Commercial Partnership" or "CP"), revenue grew 19% year over year.
L169:   * Operating margin was 32.2%; delivered an Adjusted EBITDA margin of 35.5%, up 1,060 basis points year over year. Excluding the CP, Adjusted EBITDA margin expanded 1,340 basis points year over year, marking the ninth consecutive quarter of margin expansion.
L170:   * Net income per diluted share was $0.50; generated Adjusted diluted earnings per share of $0.53, up 61% year over year, representing the ninth consecutive quarter of growth. Excluding the CP, Adjusted EPS (diluted) increased 89% year over year.

L172:   * Announced the entry into a definitive agreement to acquire 【73†RealSense, Inc.†edge.prnewswire.com】, a leader in depth-sensing cameras and vision technology for robotic perception and physical artificial intelligence (AI). The all-cash transaction is expected to close during the fourth quarter of 2026.
L173:   * Announced the launch of the In-Sight^{®} 1750, a next-generation wafer and panel identification system designed for the Semiconductor industry, one of the most demanding automation markets.
L174:   * Cognex to host 2027 Investor Day in June at its Natick, MA headquarters.

L176: "We believe we are strengthening our leadership in AI-powered machine vision through innovation, expanding our long-term growth opportunity through the announced acquisition of RealSense, and continuing to help position Cognex to benefit from what we see as powerful trends in automation, robotics, and artificial intelligence."
