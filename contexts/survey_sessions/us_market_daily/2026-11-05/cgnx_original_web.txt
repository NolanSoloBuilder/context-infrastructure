L465: NATICK, Mass., Nov. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- 【284†Cognex Corporation†edge.prnewswire.com】 (NASDAQ: 【285†CGNX】), the global technology leader in industrial machine vision, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended October 4, 2026.
L466: 
L467: Third-Quarter Financial and Operating Highlights
L468:   * Achieved record quarterly revenue of $313 million, an increase of 13% year over year, driven by broad-based strength across most major end markets; excluding the Q3 2025 one-time benefit from the commercial partnership with a medical lab automation channel partner (the "Commercial Partnership" or "CP"), revenue grew 19% year over year.
L469:   * Operating margin was 32.2%; delivered an Adjusted EBITDA margin of 35.5%, up 1,060 basis points year over year. Excluding the CP, Adjusted EBITDA margin expanded 1,340 basis points year over year, marking the ninth consecutive quarter of margin expansion.
L470:   * Net income per diluted share was $0.50; generated Adjusted diluted earnings per share of $0.53, up 61% year over year, representing the ninth consecutive quarter of growth. Excluding the CP, Adjusted EPS (diluted) increased 89% year over year.
L471:   * Increased full-year 2026 guidance; continue to anticipate strong double-digit revenue growth and year-over-year expansion in profitability.
