L525:   * Revenue was $313 million, compared with $277 million in the second quarter of 2025, an increase of 13%, driven by broad-based strength across most major end markets. Excluding the one-time benefit from the Commercial Partnership, revenue grew 19% year over year. Foreign currency exchange did not have a meaningful impact on consolidated revenue in the quarter.
L526:   * Gross margin was 70.8% compared to 67.6% in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted gross margin was 71.3% compared to 68.4% in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 290 basis points. The year-over-year increase was primarily driven by favorable mix, higher volume, and faster-than-expected pricing execution. These benefits more than offset the expected memory cost headwinds in the quarter.
L527:   * Operating expenses were $121 million compared to $130 million in the third quarter of 2025, a decrease of 7%. Adjusted operating expenses were $116 million compared to $125 million in the third quarter of 2025, a decrease of 7%, primarily reflecting continued cost management activities, somewhat offset by higher sales commissions.
L528:   * Operating income was $101 million compared to $58 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 74%. Operating margin was 32.2% compared to 20.9% in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 1,130 basis points. Adjusted operating margin was 34.2% compared to 23.2% in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 1,100 basis points.
L529:   * Adjusted EBITDA was $111 million compared to $69 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 61%. Adjusted EBITDA margin was 35.5% compared to 24.9% in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 1,060 basis points. Excluding the one-time benefit from the Commercial Partnership, Adjusted EBITDA margin increased 1,340 basis points year over year, driven by revenue growth, strong Adjusted Gross Margin and disciplined cost management.
L530:   * Net income of $85 million compared to $18 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 372%. Adjusted net income of $90 million compared to $56 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 61%.
L531:   * Net income per diluted share was $0.50 compared to $0.10 in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 400%. Adjusted diluted earnings per share were $0.53 compared to $0.33 in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 61%. Excluding the one-time benefit from the Commercial Partnership, Adjusted diluted earnings per share increased 89% year over year.

L533: Balance Sheet and Cash Flow Highlights
L534:   * As of October 4, 2026, Cognex's financial position remained strong, with $846 million in cash and investments and no debt.
L535: 
L536:   * During the third quarter, Cognex generated $106 million of cash from operating activities compared to $87 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 22%.

Third quarter FCF conversion rate was 122% of net income and 116% of Adjusted net income. Trailing twelve-month FCF conversion rate was 118% of net income and 106% of Adjusted net income.

L1230: In addition to reporting financial results in accordance with U.S. GAAP, the Company also provides various non-GAAP measures that incorporate adjustments for the impacts of special items. L1231: 
L1232: Depreciation:
L1233: 
L1234:   * The company incurs expense related to its normal use of property, plant and equipment.
L1235: 
L1236: Acquisition and integration costs:
L1237:   * The Company has incurred charges related to the purchase and integration of acquired businesses. During the periods presented, these costs were primarily related to the ongoing integration of Moritex Corporation, which the company acquired in the fourth quarter of 2023.
L1238: 
L1239: Amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets:
L1240:   * The Company excludes the amortization of acquired intangible assets from non-GAAP expense and income measures. These items are inconsistent in amount and frequency and are significantly impacted by the timing and size of acquisitions, and include the amortization of customer relationships, completed technologies, and trademarks that originated from prior acquisitions. The largest driver of intangible asset amortization was the acquisition of Moritex Corporation.
L1241: 
L1242: Reorganization charges:
L1243:   * The Company has incurred charges related to the reorganization of its employees. During the three-month period ended October 4, 2026, these costs consisted primarily of severance and consulting fees.
L1244: 
L1245: Loss on sale of business:
L1246: 
L1247:   * The Company has recognized a pre-tax loss related to the divestiture of its Japan-focused trading business, which includes direct costs associated with the divestiture incurred during the nine-month period ended October 4, 2026.
L1248: Losses on sale of investments to fund acquisition:
L1249: 
L1250:   * The Company has recognized a pre-tax loss related to the sale of investments, primarily corporate bonds, during the three-month period ended October 4, 2026. The proceeds from these sales, along with existing cash balances, are intended to fund the acquisition of RealSense that is expected to close in the fourth quarter of 2026.
L1251: 
L1252: Discrete tax (benefit) expense and tax impact of reconciling items:
L1253:   * Items unrelated to current period ordinary income or (loss) that generally relate to changes in tax laws, adjustments to prior period's actual liability determined upon filing tax returns, adjustments to previously recorded reserves for uncertain tax positions, establishments and adjustments of valuation allowances, stock based compensation, and adjustments to deferred tax positions.
L1254:   * We estimate the tax effect of items identified in the reconciliation by applying the statutory tax rate to the pre-tax amount.
